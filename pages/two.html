Our People

Creating a thriving, forward-thinking environment

Continuous innovation and creativity requires motivated, talented, and dedicated employees.

Meketa is committed to maintaining an atmosphere where individuals are treated fairly, professional growth is encouraged, and balance between work and home life is respected.

Within this framework, we provide a challenging, stimulating, team‑oriented work environment that brings out the best in our staff.

We advocate for unique ideas, which is why we value our employees’ diverse backgrounds.

We empower everyone to utilize their expertise and intellectual curiosity to contribute to the firm’s success.

I enjoy being part of a community that values results and collegiality.

LaRoy Brantley, Managing Principal / Consultant
